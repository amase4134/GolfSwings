# Name head speed

Add club-head speed to a processed clip’s filename. Do not choose the club. Do not rename a clip that already has its speed, and do not rename other existing clips just to tidy them.

## Read it

- Simulator metric labeled Head Speed.
- Numbers run from 0.0 to 250.0 mph.
- On these videos it is on the bottom of the screen, the 3rd metric from the left.
- His body blocks part of the screen during the swing. He often steps aside afterward so the numbers are fully visible. Prefer a frame after the swing when he has moved away.
- When he does not fully move, still do your best to read that same slot. Use the visible digits of the 3rd-from-left bottom metric. Do not substitute a neighboring number.

A stable 0.0 after the motion is not a head speed to write onto the name.

## Filename

Append `_{speed}mph` before `.mp4`, one decimal, matching clips he already named (example `110.5mph`). Do not add a club.

- If the file already ends in that speed, leave it.
- If the speed cannot be read, leave the filename unchanged and say so.

Do not commit videos, manifests, or `.DS_Store`.
